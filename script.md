# Forecast for Nowhere: broadcast scripts (original writing)

1 VELLOW SOUND, 3.2 MHz, Maeve
This is Vellow Sound, with the forecast at the turn of the tide. Wind: southwest, four or five, backing south later. Rain, then showers. Visibility: moderate, becoming good. The ferry did not come again today. We have left the lamp lit on the slipway, in case. If anyone is listening on Saltglass, thank you for the bottle. We read the note. We are still here.

2 THE CINDER REACH, 4.7 MHz, Fraser
Cinder Reach. Wind: hot, from the mountain, force three. Ashfall: slight, settling on the north fields. Sea state: steaming near the black beach. Visibility: orange. The mountain coughed twice at dawn and then went quiet, like it was listening. Someone out there keeps saying our name. Not a ship. A voice. Every night, at the same hour, on a frequency we don't own.

3 MARROW ISLE, 6.1 MHz, Elodie
Good evening from Marrow Isle. Wind: none, again. The apples are falling upward this week, so please mind your heads on the way to the orchard. Sea state: calm, with a slight smell of cinnamon. Visibility: we can see all the way to tomorrow, and it looks fine. We picked up a broadcast from the Cinder Reach. They sound lonely. We are sending them a crate.

4 LITTLE HUSH, 7.4 MHz, Una (whisper)
Little Hush. Please keep your voice down. Wind: holding its breath. Sea state: hush, becoming hush. Visibility: good, in the dark. At night, when everyone is asleep, we can hear every island at once, the whole sea talking over itself. And underneath them all, very faint, there is a man counting. One, two, three, four, five, six. As if he is checking we are all still here.

5 SALTGLASS, 8.8 MHz, Desmond
Saltglass forecast. Wind: northerly, six, ringing the chimes. Sea state: rough, but clear as a window. Visibility: perfect. We can see the lamp on Vellow Sound from the furnace roof. We sent them a bottle with a message. We sent one to the rock with no name as well, the one with the light. It came back unopened. That is the third time. Furnaces lit till dawn.

6 FATHOM BANK, 10.3 MHz, Grady
This is Fathom Bank, broadcasting from below. Wind: not applicable. Sea state: above us. Visibility: green, then dark, then green. We went under in the long storm, all of us, gently, like a held breath. We are fine. The fish are polite. We only broadcast so that someone keeps our name on the chart. If you are the keeper, you can stop worrying now.

7 THE KEEPER, between stations (hidden), Cillian
This is the rock with no name. Light: lit. Wind: whatever it likes. There are no islands out there. I made them up, one for every night the sea was too empty to look at. Vellow, and Cinder, and Marrow, and Hush, and Saltglass, and the one that sank. I read their weather so the sea would have somewhere to be going. And now you've been listening. So I suppose they're real enough. Thank you. Goodnight, all stations.
